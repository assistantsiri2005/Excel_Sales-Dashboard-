# 📊 Excel Sales Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing sales performance using Microsoft Excel. The dashboard summarizes sales data and presents key business metrics through visualizations and PivotTables.

The goal is to make sales performance easier to understand and help identify trends across months, countries, regions, and sales channels.

## Objectives

- Analyze overall revenue and gross profit.
- Track monthly sales performance.
- Compare revenue across countries and regions.
- Understand the performance of different sales channels.
- Present key metrics in an interactive dashboard.

## Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Excel
- Excel Tables
- PivotTables
- Charts and Data Visualization
- Sales Data Analysis

## Dataset

The workbook contains 2,707 sales records and includes fields such as:

- Order Date
- Country and Region
- Sales Channel
- Customer Type
- Product Category and Product
- Revenue, Cost of Goods Sold (COGS), and Gross Profit
- Units Sold

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- **Total Revenue:** 645,223.18
- **Total Gross Profit:** 296,384.25
- **Total COGS:** 348,838.93
- **Gross Profit Margin:** 45.94%
- **Total Units Sold:** 6,698
- **Sales Records:** 2,707

*Note: Values are reported in the dataset's currency units.*

## Key Business Insights

1. **Monthly performance:** June recorded the highest revenue among the months shown, at 69,504.34.
2. **Sales channel performance:** Online Store generated the highest revenue among the listed sales channels, at 260,307.06.
3. **Regional performance:** Western Europe recorded revenue of 198,040.41, while Central Europe recorded 191,158.66.
4. **Country performance:** The United Kingdom generated 74,427.37 in revenue, followed by Germany at 70,034.11.
5. **Profitability:** The overall gross profit margin was approximately 45.94%.

These findings help identify strong-performing periods, markets, and sales channels for further investigation.
